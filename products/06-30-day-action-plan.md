# 30-Day Action Plan
## Your Day-by-Day Guide to Your First Dollar With AI

---

## How to Use This Plan

- Do ONE day's tasks each day. Don't skip ahead.
- Each day takes 1-2 hours. Consistency beats intensity.
- Check off each task as you complete it.
- By Day 30, you'll have a live, income-generating setup.

---

## WEEK 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1-7)

### Day 1: Choose Your Path
- [ ] Read the AI Income Playbook (Methods 1-7)
- [ ] Pick ONE method that excites you most
- [ ] Write down: "I'm going to make money by [METHOD]"
- [ ] Set up a dedicated folder on your computer for this project

### Day 2: Set Up Your AI Tools
- [ ] Create a ChatGPT account (free tier is fine)
- [ ] Create a Canva account (free)
- [ ] Create a Notion account (free) for project management
- [ ] Test 5 prompts from the Prompt Library on ChatGPT
- [ ] Save the best outputs

### Day 3: Research Your Niche
- [ ] Pick a specific niche within your chosen method
- [ ] Search Google Trends for 5 related topics
- [ ] Find 3 competitors and note what they sell + their prices
- [ ] Browse Reddit/Quora — save 10 questions people are asking
- [ ] Write down the #1 problem your niche audience has

### Day 4: Define Your First Offer
- [ ] Decide: What will you sell or offer first?
- [ ] Set your price (start at $15-$27 for products, $50-$100/hr for services)
- [ ] Write a one-sentence description of your offer
- [ ] List 5 benefits your customer gets
- [ ] Identify your ideal customer (age, job, pain point)

### Day 5: Create Your Online Presence
- [ ] Set up a Gumroad account (for products) or create a simple portfolio page
- [ ] Optimize your LinkedIn profile with your new service/offering
- [ ] Create or update your Twitter/X bio with what you do
- [ ] Write a pinned post announcing what you offer

### Day 6: Build Your First Asset
- If selling a product: Create the outline using AI + start drafting
- If freelancing: Create 2 sample pieces for your portfolio
- [ ] Spend 2 hours creating. Use AI for the first draft, then edit.
- [ ] Save your work — you'll refine it tomorrow

### Day 7: Week 1 Review
- [ ] Review what you've built so far
- [ ] Refine your product/samples based on fresh eyes
- [ ] Set 3 specific goals for Week 2
- [ ] Post on social media about what you're building (accountability)

---

## WEEK 2: CREATE & LAUNCH (Days 8-14)

### Day 8: Finish Your Product/Portfolio
- [ ] Complete your digital product OR finish your portfolio samples
- [ ] Format it professionally (use Canva for design)
- [ ] Get feedback from 1-2 people you trust
- [ ] Make revisions based on feedback

### Day 9: Write Your Sales Copy
- [ ] Use Prompt #11 (Landing Page Copy) or #35 (Gumroad Listing)
- [ ] Write your product/service description
- [ ] Add: headline, pain points, benefits, what's included, price, guarantee
- [ ] Edit the AI output to sound like you

### Day 10: Set Up Payments
- [ ] List your product on Gumroad (or your platform of choice)
- [ ] OR set up your freelancing profiles (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra)
- [ ] Test the checkout/purchase flow yourself
- [ ] Make sure delivery/access works correctly

### Day 11: Create Content (Batch Day)
- [ ] Write 5 social media posts about your niche topic
- [ ] Create 2 short-form videos (Reels/TikToks) sharing tips
- [ ] Write 1 LinkedIn post positioning yourself as knowledgeable
- [ ] Schedule everything using Buffer (free)

### Day 12: Launch!
- [ ] Post your product/service announcement on all platforms
- [ ] Send a personal message to 10 people who might be interested
- [ ] Share in 3 relevant communities (Reddit, Facebook groups, Discord)
- [ ] Update your pinned posts with links

### Day 13: Follow Up & Engage
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM
- [ ] Send follow-up messages to people who showed interest
- [ ] Post a "behind the scenes" or "making of" story
- [ ] If freelancing: send 5 cold outreach emails (Template #1)

### Day 14: Week 2 Review
- [ ] Check your stats: views, clicks, sales
- [ ] What content got the most engagement?
- [ ] Adjust your approach based on data
- [ ] Celebrate any wins, no matter how small

---

## WEEK 3: GROW & OPTIMIZE (Days 15-21)

### Day 15: Double Down on What Works
- [ ] Identify your best-performing content from Week 2
- [ ] Create 3 more pieces in a similar style
- [ ] If you got sales: ask buyers for a testimonial
- [ ] If no sales yet: ask 3 people why they didn't buy (learn from objections)

### Day 16: SEO & Long-Form Content
- [ ] Write a 1,500-word blog post about your niche topic (use Prompt #1)
- [ ] Publish on Medium, LinkedIn, or your own blog
- [ ] Optimize with keywords your audience searches for
- [ ] Link back to your product/service

### Day 17: Collaborate
- [ ] Find 3 people in complementary niches
- [ ] Reach out for a collaboration (guest post, joint post, shoutout)
- [ ] Comment genuinely on 10 posts from accounts in your niche
- [ ] Join 2 new relevant online communities

### Day 18: Email List Setup
- [ ] Create a ConvertKit account (free, 1,000 contacts)
- [ ] Create a lead magnet (use Prompt #33) — a free checklist or cheat sheet
- [ ] Set up a simple landing page for your lead magnet
- [ ] Share the lead magnet link on social media

### Day 19: Content Batch Day
- [ ] Create 7 social media posts (one week's worth)
- [ ] Write 2 email drafts for your future list
- [ ] Create 1 carousel or infographic about your topic
- [ ] Film 2 short videos

### Day 20: Outreach Day
- [ ] Send 10 cold outreach messages (if freelancing)
- [ ] Apply to 5 relevant job posts on Upwork/Fiverr
- [ ] Share your product in 3 new communities
- [ ] Connect with 10 new people in your niche on LinkedIn/Twitter

### Day 21: Week 3 Review
- [ ] Review all metrics: followers, email signups, sales, responses
- [ ] What's your conversion bottleneck? (traffic? copy? price?)
- [ ] Plan adjustments for Week 4
- [ ] Write a social media post sharing your 3-week journey

---

## WEEK 4: SCALE & SYSTEMATIZE (Days 22-30)

### Day 22: Improve Your Best Seller
- [ ] Update your product/service based on customer feedback
- [ ] Improve your sales page copy (test a new headline)
- [ ] Add more social proof (testimonials, stats)
- [ ] Consider raising your price by $5-$10

### Day 23: Create a Second Offer
- [ ] Brainstorm a complementary product or service
- [ ] Outline it and create a quick version
- [ ] This gives you: a product lineup and upsell opportunities

### Day 24: Paid Traffic Experiment
- [ ] Set aside $10-$20 for a test
- [ ] Create a Facebook/Instagram ad for your product (use Prompt #13)
- [ ] Target your ideal audience narrowly
- [ ] Run for 3-5 days, track results

### Day 25: Automate
- [ ] Set up a post-purchase email sequence (use Prompt #40)
- [ ] Automate social media posting for the next 2 weeks
- [ ] Create a Zapier automation for one repetitive task
- [ ] Document your daily routine as a system

### Day 26: Partnership Outreach
- [ ] Identify 5 potential affiliate partners
- [ ] Send partnership pitch emails (use Prompt #37)
- [ ] Offer 30-50% commission for referrals
- [ ] Set up an affiliate tracking system (Gumroad has built-in)

### Day 27: Create a Content System
- [ ] Decide on a consistent posting schedule you can maintain
- [ ] Create content templates you can reuse weekly
- [ ] Batch-create 2 weeks of content in advance
- [ ] Schedule everything

### Day 28: Build a Funnel
- [ ] Map out: Free content → Lead magnet → Email → Sale
- [ ] Ensure each piece of content links to the next step
- [ ] Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers
- [ ] Test the full flow yourself

### Day 29: Review & Plan Next 90 Days
- [ ] Calculate total revenue for the month
- [ ] Calculate total time invested
- [ ] What worked? What didn't? What surprised you?
- [ ] Use Prompt #50 to set 90-day goals

### Day 30: Commit to the Long Game
- [ ] Write down your 3-month income goal
- [ ] Break it into weekly targets
- [ ] Schedule tomorrow's tasks
- [ ] Post a "30-day results" update on social media
- [ ] YOU DID IT. Most people quit. You didn't.

---

## After Day 30

You now have:
- A live product or service generating income
- A content system that runs on autopilot
- An email list growing daily
- A clear path to scale

**The next 30 days:** Double down on what works. Cut what doesn't. Create more products. Grow your audience. The compound effect kicks in around month 2-3.

**Remember:** The people making $5,000-$10,000/month with AI didn't get there overnight. They got there by doing what you just did — for 90 days straight.

Keep going.
