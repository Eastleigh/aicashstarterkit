## Template 1: Cold Outreach Email (High-Converting)

**Subject:** Quick idea for [COMPANY NAME]'s content

Hi [NAME],

I noticed [SPECIFIC THING — e.g., "your blog hasn't been updated since March" or "your Instagram posts are getting great engagement but your website copy could convert better"].

I help [TYPE OF BUSINESS] with [SERVICE] using AI-powered workflows, which means I deliver faster and at better rates than traditional agencies.

Would a 15-minute call make sense to explore if I can help? No pitch — just a quick chat to see if there's a fit.

[YOUR NAME]

**Why it works:** Specific observation + credibility + low-commitment CTA

## Template 2: Upwork Proposal (Winning Format)

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

I read your project description carefully — you need [RESTATE THEIR NEED IN YOUR WORDS].

Here's how I'd approach this:

**Phase 1:** [FIRST STEP — e.g., "Research your competitors and target keywords"]
**Phase 2:** [SECOND STEP — e.g., "Draft 5 blog posts optimized for SEO"]
**Phase 3:** [THIRD STEP — e.g., "Revise based on your feedback + format for publishing"]

I've done similar work for [TYPE OF CLIENT], and the result was [SPECIFIC OUTCOME — e.g., "a 40% increase in organic traffic within 3 months"].

I can start immediately and deliver the first draft within [TIMEFRAME].

Happy to answer any questions.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]

**Tips:** Always reference their specific project. Never send a generic proposal.

## Template 3: Pricing Calculator

| Service | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|---------|-------|----------|---------|
| Blog Posts (per post) | $75 (800 words) | $150 (1,500 words + SEO) | $300 (2,500 words + research + images) |
| Social Media Management (per month) | $400 (3 posts/week) | $800 (5 posts/week + stories) | $1,500 (daily + engagement) |
| Email Copywriting (per email) | $50 (plain text) | $100 (designed + A/B subject lines) | $200 (full sequence of 5) |
| Landing Page Copy | $150 (single page) | $350 (page + 3 variations) | $750 (page + email sequence + ads) |
| Ad Copy (per set of 5) | $100 (text only) | $250 (text + image briefs) | $500 (text + images + A/B testing plan) |

**How to price yourself:**
- Start at the "Basic" level to build reviews
- Move to "Standard" after 5 completed projects
- Offer "Premium" once you have testimonials with specific results

## Template 4: Client Onboarding Questionnaire

Send this after a client says yes:

```
Welcome aboard! To get started, I need a few things:

1. What's the main goal for this project? (e.g., more traffic, more sales, brand awareness)
2. Who is your target audience? (age, interests, pain points)
3. Do you have brand guidelines? (colors, tone of voice, logos)
4. What competitors do you admire? (links to 2-3)
5. What does success look like to you? (specific metric if possible)
6. Timeline: when do you need the first deliverable?
7. Who should I send deliverables to? (name + email)
8. Anything else I should know?

I'll have a project plan back to you within 24 hours.
```
